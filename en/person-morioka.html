Kasho Morioka

Ceramic artist / Kasho Kiln

A ceramic artist at Kasho Kiln in Shigaraki. Together with Kiriko Hitachi, who oversees winemaking and operations at KIFUTATO WINES in Hara Village, Nagano, he is contributing to ENSOU PROJECT, in which KIFUTATO WINES makes orange wine aged in a bespoke amphora from Kasho Kiln. In October 2026, he delivered the finished 20-litre, egg-shaped amphora to Hara Village.
