Kiriko Hitachi

KIFUTATO WINES / Winemaking and winery operations

At family-run KIFUTATO WINES in Hara Village, Nagano, Kiriko Hitachi oversees winemaking and winery operations. Building on the grapes cultivated at her parents' farm and experience gained at wineries in Japan and abroad, she made the family's first estate-produced wine in 2024. She is part of ENSOU PROJECT, creating orange wine at KIFUTATO WINES and ageing it in a specially crafted amphora by Kasho Kiln in Shigaraki.
